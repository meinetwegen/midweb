What is Persona 5?

Persona 5 is a Japanese role-playing game developed by ATLUS. You play a transfer student, codenamed Joker, who leads a secret group of high-school students in Tokyo. By day they attend classes and build friendships; by night they enter the Metaverse and steal the distorted desires of corrupt adults. This fan site is a short guide to the team, their Palaces and their style.

  • Genre: role-playing game with turn-based battles
  • Setting: modern Tokyo, with a hidden world called the Metaverse
  • Theme: rebellion against unfair authority

How a heist works

  1. Find a target with a distorted heart.
  2. Explore their Palace and locate the treasure.
  3. Send a calling card to announce the heist.
  4. Steal the treasure and force a change of heart.

Infiltrate

Sneak past guards, use cover and find the safest route to the treasure.

Ambush

Strike from the shadows to start a fight with the upper hand.

All-Out Attack

Knock down every enemy, then rush in together for a finishing blow.

Wanna ask some questions?

Joker is the silent leader of the team and the main character of the game. His codename comes from his ability to switch between many Personas in battle.

A Palace is a mental world shaped by one person's desires. Its ruler sees it as a castle, a museum or a bank, depending on how they see the world.
