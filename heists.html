PALACE SPECIFICATIONS

Kamoshida's Palace

Distortion Level: CRITICAL. Located at Shujin Academy. Target perceives the school as his personal castle and students as his subjects. Infiltration requires high agility to bypass heavy guard patrols and trap mechanisms.

Museum of Vanity

Distortion Level: HIGH. Located in the traditional art district. The palace is an impenetrable museum filled with stolen artwork. Infrared security lasers and frame-warping portals guard the treasure vault.

Bank of Gluttony

Distortion Level: HIGH. Floating high above Shibuya. The palace functions as a massive, heavily armored bank vault. Bypassing security requires deciphering PIN codes left behind by security chief shadows.
